About Dreamboard

DREAMBOARD is a production planning tool built for music video directors, creatives, and visual teams who need a clean way to organize projects from concept to completion.

Use it to track music video details, plan scenes, manage equipment, and keep each production organized inside one cinematic workspace.

Projects

Create and manage music video productions with artist details, shoot dates, statuses, and deliverables.

Scenes

Break each project into individual scenes with locations, moods, notes, and creative direction.

Equipment

Build an equipment vault and attach the right gear to each upcoming production.
